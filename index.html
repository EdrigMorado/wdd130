Mexico City, Mexico

Mexico City is the capital and largest city of Mexico, known for its rich history, vibrant culture, and dynamic urban life. Built on the site of the ancient Aztec capital Tenochtitlán, the city blends centuries-old traditions with modern architecture and global influence. It is one of the most populous metropolitan areas in the world and serves as Mexico’s political, economic, and cultural center. Visitors can explore historic landmarks such as the Zócalo, world-class museums, colonial architecture, and lively neighborhoods filled with food, art, and music, all surrounded by mountains and volcanoes in the Valley of Mexico.
